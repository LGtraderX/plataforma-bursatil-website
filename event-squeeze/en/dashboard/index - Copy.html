LGX Event Squeeze™ Dashboard

This is your execution framework for the World Cup 2026 opportunity.

Phase 1 — Positioning (Before Demand)

  • Do NOT wait for the crowd
  • Prepare your space early (clean, organized, functional)
  • Take clear photos before demand spikes
  • Understand your type of asset (room, basement, full house)

Early positioning gives you pricing power.

Phase 2 — Pricing Strategy

  • Start above average pricing (you can always adjust)
  • Do NOT anchor to normal market prices
  • Event demand changes the rules
  • Increase price as dates get closer

The biggest mistake is underpricing early.

Phase 3 — Demand Window

  • Expect demand spikes around key match dates
  • Short stays = lower value
  • Bundles = higher revenue
  • Control your availability strategically

You are not renting nights. You are selling access to an event.

Phase 4 — Execution

  • Respond fast to inquiries
  • Select guests carefully
  • Set clear house rules
  • Keep control of your asset

Cash flow without control = risk.

Final Principle

The difference is not the property

The difference is the strategy
