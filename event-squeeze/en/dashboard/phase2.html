Phase 2 — Pricing Strategy

Pricing is where most homeowners destroy their opportunity.

They don’t lose because of location. They don’t lose because of space. They lose because they think this is a normal market.

It is not.

This is a compressed demand event. And compressed demand changes pricing behavior completely.

Let’s remove confusion:

  • Normal markets reward competitive pricing
  • Event markets reward strategic pricing

If you price like a normal host, you will get normal results. If you price like an operator, you capture the event.

The Core Mistake

Most people anchor to what they see today.

They check Airbnb. They compare listings. They average prices.

And then they underprice their asset.

Why? Because they are looking at a market that has not moved yet.

They are pricing the past, not the future.

How You Should Think

You are not pricing a room. You are pricing access to a high-demand event.

That means:

  • Demand will be emotional
  • Availability will shrink quickly
  • Late buyers will pay higher prices

Your job is not to compete. Your job is to position above the market before it moves.

Execution Framework

  • Start above average pricing — not below
  • Do not try to “get booked fast”
  • Let demand come to you
  • Adjust based on response, not fear
  • Increase pricing as dates approach

You are testing the market, not chasing it.

Psychology of Pricing

Cheap listings attract volume. Strategic pricing attracts value.

If you price too low, you fill fast — and regret later. If you price correctly, you control who books and how much you make.

Speed is not the goal. Control is.

You can always lower your price.

You can never recover money lost from underpricing.
